# Northwind Analytics Incident Response Plan

Fictional company for the AgentForge demo. CC-BY-4.0.

## Severity Levels

Incidents are classified from SEV1 (critical) to SEV4 (low). A SEV1 incident is one that affects the confidentiality of customer data or causes a full outage of the platform.

## Detection and Triage

Security incidents are triaged within one hour of detection. A SEV1 incident is acknowledged by the on-call incident commander within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day.

## Customer Notification

Customers affected by a confirmed breach of their personal data are notified within seventy-two hours of confirmation. Notifications describe what happened, what data was affected, and what Northwind is doing in response.

## Review and Exercises

A written post-incident review is completed within five business days after every SEV1 and SEV2 incident. Northwind runs incident response tabletop exercises twice a year, and the plan is updated with the lessons learned.
