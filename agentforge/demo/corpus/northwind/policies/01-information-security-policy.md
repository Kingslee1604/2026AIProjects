# Northwind Analytics Information Security Policy

Fictional company for the AgentForge demo. CC-BY-4.0.

## Purpose and Scope

This policy defines how Northwind Analytics, Inc. protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of customer data and company systems. It applies to all employees, contractors, and systems that store or process data for the Northwind Insights platform.

## Governance

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) owns the security program and reports to the Chief Executive Officer. A Security Steering Committee of the CISO, the CTO, the VP of Engineering, and the General Counsel meets quarterly to review risks, incidents, and the progress of the security roadmap.

All security policies are reviewed at least annually by the CISO and approved by the Chief Executive Officer. Exceptions to any policy must be documented, approved by the CISO, and expire after no more than twelve months.

## Risk Management

Northwind performs a formal information security risk assessment every year and whenever a significant change is made to the platform. Risks are recorded in a risk register with an owner, a treatment plan, and a target date.

## Compliance and Assurance

Northwind maintains a SOC 2 Type II report covering the Security, Availability, and Confidentiality trust services criteria. The audit is performed annually by an independent CPA firm, and the current report is available to customers under a non-disclosure agreement.

Northwind is pursuing ISO/IEC 27001 certification, with certification targeted for 2027. Northwind does not currently hold an ISO/IEC 27001 certificate.

## Hosting

The Northwind Insights platform is hosted on Amazon Web Services. The primary region is us-east-1 and the disaster recovery region is us-west-2. Customers on the Enterprise plan may choose EU data residency, in which case customer data is stored in eu-central-1.
