# Northwind Analytics Personnel Security and Acceptable Use Policy

Fictional company for the AgentForge demo. CC-BY-4.0.

## Hiring

Background checks are performed on all new employees before their start date, where permitted by local law. All employees and contractors sign a confidentiality agreement before receiving access to company systems.

## Security Awareness

All employees complete security awareness training during onboarding and every year after that. Phishing simulation exercises are run every quarter.

## Acceptable Use

Company devices and accounts may be used only for authorized business purposes. Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal accounts.

## Physical Security

Northwind relies on the physical security controls of its cloud provider's data centers. Northwind offices use badge access, and visitors are escorted at all times.
