# Northwind Analytics Data Retention and Privacy Policy

Fictional company for the AgentForge demo. CC-BY-4.0.

## Data Minimization

Northwind collects only the personal data needed to provide the Northwind Insights platform. Northwind does not sell customer data or use it for advertising.

## Retention and Deletion

Customer data is deleted within 30 days after a customer's contract ends. Copies in backups expire on their own within 35 days of deletion. Customers can export their data at any time before deletion.

## Privacy Commitments

Northwind signs a data processing agreement (DPA) with every customer. For transfers of personal data out of the European Economic Area, the DPA incorporates the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Data Subject Requests

Requests from customers to access, correct, or delete personal data are completed within 30 days.
