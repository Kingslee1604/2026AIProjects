# Northwind Analytics Vendor Management Policy

Fictional company for the AgentForge demo. CC-BY-4.0.

## Due Diligence

Every vendor that will access company or customer data is assessed before a contract is signed. Vendors are assigned a risk tier of critical, high, or standard based on the data they access.

## Ongoing Review

Critical vendors are reviewed every year, and the review includes their SOC 2 report or an equivalent independent audit. Contracts with vendors that process customer data include data protection and confidentiality terms.

## Subprocessors

Northwind publishes its list of subprocessors on its trust page. Customers are notified at least 30 days before a new subprocessor begins processing their data.
