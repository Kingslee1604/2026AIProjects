# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The short version is below.

## Our pledge

We want participation in AgentForge to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use and build the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere. Report problems privately to the maintainer through
GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (Security tab → "Report a vulnerability"), marking the
report as a conduct matter. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
comments, commits, or contributions, and may ban contributors, temporarily or permanently, for
behaviour they judge harmful.
